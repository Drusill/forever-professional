## ForeverProfessional 0.2.0

- **Filters:** a new Filters menu in the toolbar. Show only recipes that still give skill-ups, recipes you can learn now, recipes from a given source (trainer, vendor, drop, quest, other, unknown) or recipes new in Forever. Filters are saved, and the button shows how many are active.
- **Materials calculator:** click Calculate on any recipe, learned or not, to plan what you need.
  - Shopping: the raw materials to buy, with what you already have in your bags and bank.
  - Steps: every intermediate to craft, in order, and who can make it: you, one of your characters, or nobody.
  - Tree: the full reagent tree. Switch any intermediate between Craft and Buy; your choices are saved, and Reset choices undoes them.
  - Set a quantity in crafts or in items; recipes that make several items show how many crafts you need and the extras.
- Recipes you've already learned are never shown in red, even if your skill is below their listed level.
- Data fixes: corrected learn levels for several Tailoring, Leatherworking and Blacksmithing recipes (Orcish War Leggings, mooncloth, slippers and more), Ornate Mithril recipes are taught by their quests, and Disorienting Smog Potion is gone (it isn't in the game).
