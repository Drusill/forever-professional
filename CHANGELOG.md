## ForeverProfessional 0.1.0

First release.

- Browse all 2,159 recipes of every profession, including the 921 added in Forever, by category and in their difficulty colour. Filter by All, Learned or Missing, or search by name.
- See where to get each recipe you don't know: trainers (with cost), vendors (with reputation and Merchant's Favor requirements), quests, drops (with the drop rate when known) and chests.
- Put a map pin on an NPC or object with the Waypoint button.
- Switch between your characters to see their professions and recipes.
- Recipe item tooltips show which of your characters know the recipe, can learn it now or need more skill.
- Open it with `/fp`, the minimap button or the addon compartment.
