  • Rail: the shown character's professions first (skill under each), then a divider and the rest, smaller and greyed. The selected profession gets its colour as a left marker and glow.
  • Header: profession icon and name, its skill bar in the game's own art for that profession, recipes learned, the character selector and the close button. A faint profession illustration sits behind it.
  • Toolbar: search, All / Learned / Missing with counts, collapse all, expand all.
  • Rows: two lines. Line 1 is the name in its difficulty colour, with ✓ or the source icon on the right. Line 2 is the learn level plus where to get it (missing), or the four skill numbers (learned).
  • Detail, not learned: pinned header (icon, name, Creates, chips, skill scale), then Where to get it, Characters, Reagents. Only the part under the header scrolls.
  • Bottom-right corner: resize grip.
